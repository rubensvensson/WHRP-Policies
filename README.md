# WHRP Polismyndigheten – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the WHRP Polismyndigheten Discord bot may process information when used in the West Halmstad Roleplay (WHRP) community.

We aim to handle information responsibly and only use information necessary to operate and maintain the bot.

### 2. Information We May Process
Depending on the features being used, the bot may process:
- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server IDs.
- Discord channel and role IDs.
- Command inputs and interactions with the bot.
- Information submitted through bot forms or applications.
- Technical logs used to identify errors and maintain the service.

The exact information processed depends on which bot features are enabled.

### 3. How Information Is Used
Information may be used to:
- Provide the bot's functions.
- Process commands and applications.
- Manage roleplay-related systems.
- Maintain security and prevent misuse.
- Identify and resolve technical issues.

### 4. Storage and Retention
Some information may be stored to support bot functionality.

Information should only be retained for as long as it is needed for its intended purpose. Storage and deletion depend on the bot's configuration and the systems used to operate it.

### 5. Sharing of Information
We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services required to operate the bot, such as Discord and the bot hosting provider.

### 6. Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information and restrict access to authorized staff. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

### 7. Your Rights
Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information.

For privacy-related requests, contact the WHRP staff team through the official WHRP Discord server.

### 8. Children's Privacy
The bot is intended for use within the WHRP community. Users should not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information through the bot.

### 9. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will be made available through the official WHRP channels.

### 10. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or information processed by the bot, contact the WHRP staff team through the official WHRP Discord server.
